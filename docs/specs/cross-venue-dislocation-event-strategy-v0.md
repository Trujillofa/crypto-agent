## Lineage and what changed

The cross-venue-basis-v1 lane produced a genuine probe HAS_PULSE (27 passing scenarios
across BTC/ETH/SOL on binance_usdm−bybit spreads) but was closed after a 30-run sweep
rejected the **overlay shape** 0/30: require-mode entry gates starve the SOL 1h stack
to 0–7 WFO trades; block-mode gates engage 1–11 times in ~2 years with no risk lift.

What the sweep did **not** test — and what the probe actually measured — is trading
the dislocation event itself. This lane tests exactly that shape and nothing else.

**HYP-CVE-001:** After a cross-venue dislocation event (spread tail extreme or
normalization onset on binance_usdm−bybit basis/premium spread), price exhibits
forward drift that is exploitable **as a standalone entry**: enter at the event bar's
close in the scenario-implied direction, exit at a fixed horizon (6h/12h/24h), with
positive expectancy net of realistic taker fees and slippage.

## Why this is allowed under the reset rules

1. **Different primitive** — multi-venue derivatives microstructure event, not an
   OHLCV shape, session label, or single-venue premium tail. Not an overlay patch.
2. **Cheap probe first** — read-only probe defined below; HAS_PULSE required before
   any strategy class or autoresearch family.
3. **Standalone surface** — entries are generated by the event, never attached to the
   promoted SOL overlay.
4. **WFO-realistic event count** — tail5/tail10 of ~21k joined bars/symbol gives
   O(1000) raw tail bars; the probe must show the **deduplicated** count still
   supports ≥20 WFO trades per fold (see kill criteria).
5. **Independent directionality** — `extreme_negative` scenarios on ETH/SOL imply
   short entries; the short-backtest parity infra is parked and ready. A two-sided
   event book adds directionality the long-biased live agents lack.

Banned shapes remain banned: no filter/gate variants on the SOL stack, no crowding
mean-reversion entry framing without event conditioning, no threshold iteration on
the closed overlay lane.

## Gate 1 — Sharpened cheap probe (the deliverable of this lane's first step)

New read-only script `scripts/probe_dislocation_event_strategy.py` (may import
helpers from `scripts/probe_basis_premium.py` / `scripts/probe_cross_venue_basis.py`,
but must fix the four known gaps in that machinery):

1. **No lookahead thresholds.** The v1 probe computed tail percentiles over the full
   sample. The event probe must use either (a) trailing rolling-window percentiles
   (e.g. 90d lookback, threshold known at event time), or (b) a fixed absolute-bps
   grid declared up front (as the sweep sampler did). Both modes reported.
2. **Event deduplication.** Consecutive tail bars cluster into one event at first
   trigger; a cooldown equal to the exit horizon prevents overlapping trades. Report
   raw vs deduplicated counts per scenario.
3. **Net-of-cost returns.** Subtract round-trip taker fees (0.08%) plus a slippage
   allowance (0.02%) from every event's forward return: net = gross − 0.10%. All
   pass/fail evaluation on net numbers; gross reported alongside.
4. **Direction-aware, two-sided.** Each scenario carries an entry direction (long and
   short variants both evaluated). MAE for shorts measured against highs, not lows.

Per scenario × symbol × horizon, report: deduplicated event count, net mean/median
forward return, win rate, MAE distribution, monthly concentration (max share of
total net P&L in any single month), and per-direction breakdown.

**HAS_PULSE requires, on at least one symbol or pooled:**
- ≥40 deduplicated events over the window (and a rate consistent with ≥20 WFO
  trades per 2-month test fold when scaled);
- net mean forward return > 0 **and** median > 0 at some horizon;
- win rate ≥ 45% at that horizon;
- monthly concentration ≤ 50%;
- the result holds in the no-lookahead threshold mode (rolling or fixed grid),
  not only in the full-sample mode.

## Kill criteria (close at probe, cheaply)

- Deduplication collapses counts below 40 everywhere → frequency illusion; close.
- Net edge ≤ 0 on all dedup+fee scenarios at all horizons → fee-dominated; close.
- Edge survives only with full-sample (lookahead) thresholds → artifact; close.
- P&L concentrated in one month/episode (>50%) → single-event story; close.
- Direction flips sign across symbols with no consistent rule → no tradable rule; close.

## Gates 2–6 (RBI-aligned, unchanged from house rules)

- **Gate 2:** Bounded autoresearch family (new `dislocation_event_entry` family,
  standalone strategy, not an overlay) only after HAS_PULSE. Standard gate profile.
- **Gate 3/4:** Promotion pre-filter, then bootstrap=1000.
- **Gate 5:** `analyze_entry_overlap.py` against `sol-1h-trend-pullback-overlay-live`
  and `sentiment-macro` (target <35% Jaccard).
- **Gate 6:** ≥20 closed paper trades under a distinct AGENT_ID before any live notional.

Human approval required at every stage transition; the lane manifest guard
(`rbi_loop_from_manifest.py`, dry by default) records each decision.

## Stop / close rules

- Probe NO_PULSE or fails kill criteria → close lane, record verdict, no reshape.
- Any implementation needing >5 free parameters to pass gates → close.
- Evidence the event signal is a proxy for sentiment-macro entries → close (overlap).

Goal unchanged: a second independent, gate-passing agent. This is the cheapest
remaining test of the only HAS_PULSE evidence currently on the books.
