# Sentiment replay limitations

- Indicator `time` is the OHLCV candle **open**. Replay currently uses that timestamp, including in `execution_parity_v2`, whose strategy evaluation is described as occurring at bar close. Thus a provider observation during the candle is excluded even though it may precede a close decision. This is not a validated reconstruction of live decision time. The real decision can occur after close, indicator publication and polling; a nominal close or timeframe offset is not evidence of that actual time. Equal-time log events also lack a shared decision-order key. Do not interpret replay results as live-parity evidence until the decision clock and ordering contract are established. No live gate was changed.
- An error-source or invalid-score observation starts a replay gap and produces the configured fallback, normally 50. This is not equivalent to live provider degradation: the live scorer tracks a rolling multi-symbol window and its `degraded` flag can suppress BUY independently of the numeric score. Replay does not reproduce that state, and a neutral score is not a universal entry veto (thresholds are configurable). Do not interpret replay as a live degradation simulation. Reproducing the gate requires a defined historical observation and decision ordering, cache behavior and configuration; no substitute policy is inferred here.
- Malformed JSON or events lacking an identifiable timestamp/symbol are skipped; their effect on an unknown symbol/time cannot be reconstructed. A stream decoding or I/O failure invalidates the entire load so an earlier partial timeline cannot masquerade as complete. Replay then returns the configured fallback, which again is not necessarily a trading veto.
