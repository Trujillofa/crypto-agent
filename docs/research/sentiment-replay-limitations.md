# Sentiment replay limitations

- Indicator `time` is the OHLCV candle **open**. Replay currently uses that timestamp, including in `execution_parity_v2`, whose strategy evaluation is described as occurring at bar close. Thus a provider observation during the candle is excluded even though it may precede a close decision. This is not a validated reconstruction of live decision time. The real decision can occur after close, indicator publication and polling; a nominal close or timeframe offset is not evidence of that actual time. Equal-time log events also lack a shared decision-order key. Do not interpret replay results as live-parity evidence until the decision clock and ordering contract are established. No live gate was changed.
- **Known replay/live divergence during a provider outage.** An error-source or invalid-score observation starts a replay gap and produces the configured fallback, normally neutral 50. Live does not do that: an unusable provider answer is scored with the live error fallback of **30** and sets `degraded=True` (`src/main.py:937`), which can veto a BUY independently of the numeric score. Replay builds its scorer without that error fallback (`src/backtest/portfolio.py:129-132`), so a gap returns **50** with no `degraded` state. Consequence: replay can permit entries during an outage in which live would veto them. This divergence is a deliberate policy decision, not a replay defect; it is not changed here because the live error fallback belongs to the live scorer and to trading decisions. A replay window covering a provider outage must not be read as evidence about live entries. Reproducing the gate requires a defined historical observation and decision ordering, cache behavior and configuration; no substitute policy is inferred here.
- Every record `EventLog` writes is newline-terminated, so a final line without a trailing newline that does not decode is a torn append rather than a malformed historical row, and an error-source record whose timestamp is missing or cannot be normalized cannot be placed in time at all. Both make the newest state unknowable. Replay keeps the durable history but treats the affected symbols — every symbol for a torn tail or for a record with no symbol, the named symbol otherwise — as gapped after their newest durable observation, so an older score is never replayed as if it were current. The cost is bounded conservatism: candles strictly after the newest durable observation fall back even when that unknown record could have been a healthy score, and an error record with a broken clock cannot be confined to its true (unknown) window.
- Decodable malformed JSON or events lacking an identifiable timestamp/symbol are skipped; their effect on an unknown symbol/time cannot be reconstructed. A stream decoding or I/O failure invalidates the entire load so an earlier partial timeline cannot masquerade as complete. Replay then returns the configured fallback, which again is not necessarily a trading veto. `stats()["tainted_symbols"]` reports how many symbols were gapped for the reasons above, so a coverage log cannot present them as ordinary loaded observations.
