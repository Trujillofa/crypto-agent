================================================================================
  4. PARAMETER SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Key findings from 300-candidate grid:

a) REGIME PARAMETERS MATTER MOST
   - Strict regime thresholds (0.005 trend strength, 50+ consistency) = fewer
     trades but not better outcomes. They just avoid entries entirely.
   - Loose thresholds (0.002, 25) = more entries but into whipsawing ranges.

b) RSI IS THE DOMINANT TRADE FILTER
   - Tight RSI (45/55): 3-9 trades — too few to be statistically meaningful
   - Moderate (40/60): 8-20 trades — still negative
   - Wide (35/65): 16-44 trades — more negative, more drawdown

c) MANY PARAMETERS ARE IRRELEVANT
   - buy_threshold, trending_confidence, trend_filter produce DUPLICATE results
   - 75 candidates per strategy often collapse to only 4-12 unique outcomes
   - The grid is degenerate — most parameters don't reach the signal path

d) MORE TRADES = WORSE RESULTS (monotonically)
   - This confirms the entry logic is anti-predictive on ETHUSDT

================================================================================
  5. LOOKAHEAD BIAS VERIFICATION
================================================================================

VERIFIED: No lookahead bias present.

The backtest engine uses the IndicatorReader._join_timeframes() method with
STRICT as-of join semantics:

  Code: src/features/reader.py, line 472
  Logic: regime_close_time <= entry_time

This ensures:
  - A 4h bar that opens at 08:00 and closes at 12:00 is only available to
    1h entry bars at 12:00 or later
  - Same-timestamp availability (close_time == entry_time) is allowed,
    which is correct — the bar is fully formed at that point
  - No future regime data leaks into historical entries

================================================================================
  6. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Why MTF fails on ETHUSDT (2024-2026):

1. WRONG MARKET REGIME
   ETHUSDT has been predominantly choppy/ranging at macro scale. The MTF
   strategies are all trend-following variants that need sustained directional
   moves on 4h timeframe. ETH provides these <20% of the time.

2. ENTRY LOGIC IS COUNTER-PRODUCTIVE
   "Buy the pullback in a trend" sounds good, but in ETH's regime:
   - Pullbacks to VWAP/EMA50 during weak trends often continue as reversals
   - The 2x ATR stop loss is too tight for the volatility during trend tests
   - By the time 4h confirms a trend (high slope + consistency), the move is
     often exhausted

3. EXIT RULES DON'T COMPENSATE
   - TP at 4.5x ATR rarely gets hit in choppy trends
   - Time stop (240 min) often exits positions that haven't resolved
   - Trailing stop at 1.5x ATR activates then gets stopped out by noise

4. HIGH-FREQUENCY STRATEGIES ARE WORSE (NOT BETTER)
   mtf_continuation, mtf_breakout, and mtf_template all produce far more
   trades (100-600+) but dramatically worse outcomes (up to -94% return).
   The entry logic is actively losing money on most trades.

================================================================================
  7. RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

DO NOT deploy any MTF configuration on ETHUSDT.

To achieve >1.2 profit factor on ETH, consider:

a) ABANDON TREND-FOLLOWING FOR ETH
   - ETH in 2024-2026 is a mean-reversion market
   - Consider regime-aware mean reversion: sell strength, buy weakness
   - Bollinger bounce or VWAP reversion with regime filter may work better

b) TEST MTF ON BTCUSDT INSTEAD
   - BTC shows stronger and longer trend persistence
   - The MTF regime router may be viable on a more trending asset

c) IF CONTINUING MTF RESEARCH ON ETH
   - Widen SL to 3-4x ATR (current 2x gets whipsawed)
   - Add a bounce-confirmation filter (don't enter on touch, enter on reclaim)
   - Reduce regime timeframe to 2h or use multi-regime confirmation (1h+4h)
   - Consider inverted signals: SHORT the MTF trend entries instead of LONG

d) STRUCTURAL IMPROVEMENTS
   - Fix the parameter grid degeneracy — many params have zero effect
   - Add early termination for strategies losing >50% in backtest
   - Profile which regime windows the losses concentrate in
